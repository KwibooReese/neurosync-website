Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: your check-ins are shared only with the person you connect with in the app. We don't sell, rent or trade your data, we don't use it for advertising, and you can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

NeuroSync is a wellbeing app that helps two people, including those with ADHD, autism, AuDHD or other neurodivergent profiles, stay in tune with each other's mental states, energy levels and needs. It is not a medical app and is not a substitute for professional mental health support.

NeuroSync is operated by Hayman Software Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17403938), whose registered office is at 71–75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ, United Kingdom. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Hayman Software Ltd. We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

This policy applies to everyone who uses the NeuroSync app on iOS or Android, and to this website.

Information we collect

Account information

  • Email address and display name (required to create an account)
  • Profile photo (optional, uploaded by you)
  • Your neurotype selection (for example ADHD, autism, AuDHD or neurotypical)
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account's email address and name

Check-in information

  • Mental state selections (for example "anxious" or "overstimulated"), including custom states you create
  • Physical and mental energy levels (numbers from 1 to 10)
  • Current need selections (for example "quiet space" or "reassurance"), including custom needs you create
  • Optional notes you add to a check-in (up to 200 characters)
  • The date and time of each check-in, and your check-in streak

Some of this information, such as your neurotype and mental states, may reveal information about your health. We treat it with extra care, as described below.

Partnership information

  • The partnership that connects you with the other person, and the invite code used to join it
  • Subscription status (active or inactive) for your partnership

Device and notification information

  • Push notification tokens, used to send you check-in and partnership notifications
  • Your device platform (iOS or Android), used to deliver notifications

Payment information

Subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat gives us your subscription status and purchase history (such as which plan you have and when it renews). See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how they handle this information.

How we use your information

  • To create and run your NeuroSync account
  • To show your check-ins to the person you're connected with
  • To send notifications, for example when your partner checks in or joins your partnership
  • To tailor app content (mental states, needs and learning content) to your neurotype
  • To provide and manage your subscription
  • To send account emails, such as email address confirmation and password resets
  • To keep the app secure and to fix problems
  • To improve the app using aggregated, non-identifying usage patterns

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Our legal basis for using your information

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), we rely on:

  • Contract: to provide the service you signed up for, including your account, check-ins, partnership and subscription.
  • Explicit consent: for information that may relate to your health, such as your neurotype and mental states. You give this consent when you choose to enter that information, and you can withdraw it at any time by deleting it or deleting your account.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the app secure, prevent misuse and improve the app using aggregated information, where this doesn't override your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or share information to comply with the law.

Who we share your information with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers. Your check-in information is shared only with the person you're connected with in the app.

We use these service providers to run NeuroSync. They process information only on our instructions, are contractually required to protect it, and may not use it for their own marketing:

  • Supabase: secure database, sign-in and file storage, where your account, check-in, partnership and profile photo data is held
  • RevenueCat: subscription management and purchase verification
  • Apple and Google: app store purchases, and push notification delivery (Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Expo: the notification service that passes notifications from NeuroSync to Apple and Google for delivery
  • Google Sign-In: an optional way to sign in
  • Resend: sending account emails, such as email address confirmation

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or where necessary to protect the safety of our users or others.

Where your information is stored

Our main database is hosted by Supabase in the United Kingdom (London). Some of our service providers, such as RevenueCat, Expo, Google and Apple, may process information in other countries, including the United States. Where information is transferred outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, standard contractual clauses or the UK–US data bridge.

App permissions

  • Photo library: to let you choose a profile photo. We only access the photo you choose.
  • Notifications: to alert you when your partner checks in or joins. You can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and check-in history within 30 days. We may keep partnership information in anonymised form, which can no longer identify you, for aggregate analysis. We may also keep limited information where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes or enforce our terms.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong
  • ask us to delete your information
  • ask us to restrict, or object to, how we use your information
  • receive your information in a portable format
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email support@neurosyncapp.com. We'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113, though we'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your NeuroSync account and all associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

In the app: go to Profile & settings and choose Delete account.

By email: email support@neurosyncapp.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request", from or including the email address linked to your account.

What gets deleted: your account information (name, email, profile photo, neurotype), your check-in history (mental states, energy levels, needs and notes), your custom states and needs, and your partnership link.

What may be kept: partnership information in anonymised form that can no longer identify you, and limited information where the law requires it.

Timeframe: we complete deletion within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in your store account settings to stop future payments.

Security

Your information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access rules in our database ensure that people can only see their own information and the check-ins of the person they're connected with. We take reasonable steps to protect against unauthorised access, but no system is completely secure.

Age limit

NeuroSync is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
