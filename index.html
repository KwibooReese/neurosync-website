How it works

Not a mood tracker. A way to explain what's going on, without having to find the words.

1

Check in

Pick how you're feeling, slide your mental and physical energy, and choose what you need. Add a short note if you like.

2

Your partner understands

They see your check-in straight away, with an explanation of what that state is actually like.

3

They know how to help

Practical guidance on what helps and what to avoid, so support doesn't come down to guesswork.

Learn together

Every check-in teaches you something about each other

Over time you stop guessing. You learn what a shutdown really is, why "just do it" doesn't help with task paralysis, and what your partner needs when they're peopled out.

The Learn tab explains every state in plain language, so you can understand them before the hard moments, not during. More lessons on ADHD and autism are on the way.

  • Shutdown Mode

    Not ignoring you — cannot respond. What it is, and how to be there without adding pressure.

  • Task Paralysis

    Knowing what to do and wanting to, but not being able to start. Why it happens and how body doubling helps.

  • Peopled Out

    The social battery is empty. Why it isn't about you, and how to give real space.

Made for neurodivergent brains

Built by someone with ADHD, for the moments that are hard to explain out loud.

States that actually fit

Everyday feelings, plus the ones generic mood apps miss: overstimulated, masking fatigue, hyperfocus, meltdown warning.

Mental and physical energy

Two simple levels, because being physically fine and mentally empty are very different days.

Say what you need

Alone time, reassurance, body doubling, quiet space. One tap, no awkward conversation.

Make it yours

Create your own states and needs, and hide the ones that don't apply to you.

Gentle streaks

A small nudge to keep checking in, without guilt when life gets busy.

Private to the two of you

Your check-ins are shared only with your partner. We never sell your data.

Questions

Who is NeuroSync for?

Couples where one or both partners have ADHD, are autistic, or both — whether you're diagnosed, self-identified or still figuring it out. It's also for neurotypical partners who want to understand.

When can I get it?

NeuroSync is coming soon to iPhone and Android. Check back here, or email support@neurosyncapp.com if you have a question in the meantime.

Do we both need the app?

Yes. One of you sets up the partnership and shares a 6-character code; the other joins with it. One subscription covers you both, and there's a free trial.

Is it a medical app?

No. NeuroSync is a communication and wellbeing tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything and isn't a substitute for professional support. If you're in crisis, call NHS 111 or Samaritans on 116 123 (UK and Ireland), or 988 in the US.

How do I get help or delete my data?

Email support@neurosyncapp.com. You can also delete your account any time from Profile & settings in the app. See our privacy policy for details.
